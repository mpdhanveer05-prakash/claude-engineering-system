## Agenda (30 min)

1. **Recap PRD** (5m) — read or skim out loud
2. **Demo target state** (5m) — designer or PM walks the desired UX
3. **Approach options** (10m) — eng proposes 1–2 paths
4. **Risks + unknowns** (5m) — surface them now
5. **Slice & commit** (5m) — first deliverable + when

## Outputs

- [ ] Approach picked (1 sentence)
- [ ] Feature flag named: `feature.<slug>`
- [ ] Slice 1 scope: ...
- [ ] Slice 1 owner: ...
- [ ] Slice 1 target date: ...
- [ ] Open questions parked in `planning/features/<slug>.md`

- The PRD already nails the approach
