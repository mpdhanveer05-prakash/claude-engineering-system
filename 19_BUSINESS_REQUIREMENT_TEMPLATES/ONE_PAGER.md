# One-Pager — {Idea}

> ≤500 words. If you can't fit, you don't understand it yet.

## The user
Who? In one sentence.

## The pain
Why is today bad for them? In one sentence.

## The shift
What changes for them after we ship? In one sentence.

## The product
What we make. In one sentence.

## The wedge
Why us, why this, why now. In one sentence.

## The metric
One number that tells us this worked.

## The non-goal
The most tempting thing we will NOT do, and why.

## The risk
The thing most likely to kill this.

## The ask
Smallest commitment to decide whether to keep going.
